QUALITY REQUIREMENTS
- This is a real, exhaustive reference grammar, not a summary. For each grammar point:
  clear explanation → structure box(es) → several examples (normally 3–6, more for central topics)
  → negative and interrogative forms when relevant → exceptions, contrasts with similar structures,
  typical mistakes of Italian learners, register (parlato/scritto) when relevant.
- Every Chinese example must be natural, idiomatic, correct Mandarin in simplified characters, with
  correct pinyin and a natural Italian translation. When it helps to understand the structure, add a
  literal gloss in the translation, e.g. «Ho letto il libro tre volte. (lett. leggere-PERF tre volte libro)».
  Use everyday, varied vocabulary and names (小王, 李老师, 玛丽亚, 马可, 王明 …).
- Double-check every pinyin syllable and tone. Pinyin conventions (strict):
  · tone marks, never tone numbers;
  · word segmentation per standard pinyin orthography (xuésheng, Zhōngguó, xǐhuan, péngyou, kàn shū,
    chī fàn); particles and measure words written separately (le, de, ma, ne, ba, zhe, guo; yí ge rén,
    sān běn shū);
  · capital letter at the start of each sentence and for proper names (Běijīng, Xiǎo Wáng, Lǐ lǎoshī);
    Western punctuation in pinyin (. , ? ! :);
  · neutral tone without any mark (de, le, ma, men, zi, xuésheng, dōngxi, rènshi, zhīdao, juéde, gàosu,
    xǐhuan, piàoliang, shíhou, yīfu);
  · the tone changes of 一 and 不 are marked as actually pronounced (yí ge, yì běn, yì tiān, yí cì,
    bú shì, bú qù, bù hǎo, kàn yi kàn, hǎo bu hǎo, qù bu qù); 一 stays yī when counting, in ordinals,
    dates and at the end of a word (dì-yī, yī yuè, tǒngyī);
  · third-tone sandhi is NOT marked (nǐ hǎo, wǒ yě hěn hǎo);
  · unstressed directional complements in neutral tone (jìnlai, chūqu, huílai, ná qilai);
  · in the potential complement 得/不 are neutral: tīng de dǒng, tīng bu dǒng.
- Italian: clear, correct, didactic; standard Italian grammatical terminology. Give the Chinese term
  (hanzi + pinyin) of a grammatical concept the first time it appears in your chapters,
  e.g. «i classificatori (量词 liàngcí)».
- In hanzi use proper Chinese punctuation (，。？！：；、“”《》……——).

FILE FORMAT (strict: the files are parsed by a script)
Plain UTF-8 text. Each line is interpreted according to how it begins:

### Titolo della sottosezione  → unnumbered sub-heading
= S + 很 + Agg                 → "Struttura" box. Consecutive "=" lines form one box (one structure per line).
ex: 我是学生。 | Wǒ shì xuésheng. | Sono uno studente.

                                 Consecutive ex:/no: lines are grouped in one block.

| Col 1 | Col 2 | Col 3 |      → table: consecutive lines starting with "|"; the FIRST row is the header.

any other line                 → normal paragraph text. Consecutive text lines are joined into one paragraph;
                                 an empty line ends the paragraph.

Inline formatting (allowed in text, notes, bullets, tables, structure boxes and inside ex:/no: fields):
**bold** and *italic* only. Inside ex: lines use **…** to highlight the grammar point in BOTH hanzi and pinyin:
ex: 我**把**书放在桌子上了。 | Wǒ **bǎ** shū fàng zài zhuōzi shang le. | Ho messo il libro sul tavolo.
Dialogues fit in one ex: line: «ex: A：你去吗？B：去。 | A: Nǐ qù ma? B: Qù. | A: Ci vai? B: Sì.»
