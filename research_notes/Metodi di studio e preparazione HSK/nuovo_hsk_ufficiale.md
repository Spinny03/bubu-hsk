## 1. Exam structure per level (HSK 1–3 in detail; HSK 4–6 and 7–9 summarized): parts, counts, question types, timing, pinyin, audio repetitions, answer-sheet time

### Takeaway
The official December 2025 structure document and the official sample papers set the format. HSK 1 has 40 items (listening + reading, about 40 min). HSK 2 has 60 items and now includes a writing (书写) section (about 60 min). HSK 3 has 70 items (about 83 min). Pinyin is printed over every character in HSK 1–2 and nowhere in HSK 3, apart from the pinyin cue above blanks in writing Part 1. After listening there is time to fill in the answer sheet: 3 min at HSK 1–2 and 5 min at HSK 3–6. Every level also includes 5 min for personal data. In the official sample audio, every HSK 1–3 listening item is played twice and HSK 4 items are played once (own measurement).

On the paper, each item has a row of 3 pictures — [CTI sample pack: HSK（一级）样卷.pdf p.2](https://hsk-sample-1409982902.cos.ap-beijing.myqcloud.com/HSK3.0.zip)
- Listening Part 2 (6–10): hear a question (e.g. "今天天气怎么样？") and choose the right reply from 3 printed text options with pinyin (A 在学习 / B 很好听 / C 非常热 → C) — [STRUTT p.3](https://confucio.unimc.it/it/certificazioni/HSK16.pdf). - Listening Part 3 (11–15): hear a short dialogue ("女：你好！男：你好，很高兴认识你！") and match it to 1 of 6 pictures A–F (the example uses one of the six) — [STRUTT pp.3–4](https://confucio.unimc.it/it/certificazioni/HSK16.pdf)
- Listening Part 4 (16–20): hear a statement plus a question ("下午我去超市，我想买一些水果。问：说话人下午去哪里？") and choose from 3 text options with pinyin (超市/医院/学校) — [STRUTT p.4](https://confucio.unimc.it/it/certificazioni/HSK16.pdf)
- Reading Part 1 (21–25): a sentence with pinyin ("我很喜欢这本书。") is matched to 1 of 6 pictures A–F — [STRUTT p.5](https://confucio.unimc.it/it/certificazioni/HSK16.pdf)
- Reading Part 2 (26–30): match questions (left) to replies A–F (right), e.g. "你喝水吗？" → "F 好的，谢谢！" — [STRUTT p.6](https://confucio.unimc.it/it/certificazioni/HSK16.pdf)
- Reading Part 3 (31–35): fill the blank from a 6-word bank A–F ("你（ D 叫 ）什么名字？") — [STRUTT p.7](https://confucio.unimc.it/it/certificazioni/HSK16.pdf)
- Reading Part 4 (36–40): read a sentence and answer a ★ question with 3 options ("请问现在几点了？ ★说话人想知道什么？ A 时间 B 名字 C 房间号") — [STRUTT p.7](https://confucio.unimc.it/it/certificazioni/HSK16.pdf)
- Pinyin is printed above every character in all HSK 1 reading texts and listening options. HSK 1 has no writing section — [STRUTT pp.2–7](https://confucio.unimc.it/it/certificazioni/HSK16.pdf). A secondary news source (上游新闻) also reports that HSK 1 tests only listening and reading, levels 2–3 add writing (书写) and levels 4–6 add composition (写作) — [163.com / 上游新闻, 20 Dec 2025](https://www.163.com/dy/article/KH818PEN053469M5.html)

### Inferences
- For a faithful simulator, an app needs to reproduce:
  - pinyin over characters at HSK 1–2 and none at HSK 3;
  - each listening item played twice at HSK 1–3 but once at HSK 4+, with the measured pauses;
  - in paper mode, the 3- or 5-minute answer-sheet transfer after listening and the 5 minutes for personal data;
  - separate timing for the writing section at HSK 2–3;
  - six-option matching sets where the example uses up one option.
- HSK 2 is the level that changed most for learners: it adds writing. Part 1 is choosing the missing component of a character; Part 2 is producing a character from its pinyin. Character-production practice (the HSK 1–2 list of 100 characters to write, see §2) therefore has to begin before HSK 2.
- HSK 3 writing in the new format is "character from pinyin" plus "sentence from picture and keyword". The 2025 samples contain no word-ordering "complete the sentence" items, which old HSK 3 writing had. That comparison comes from background knowledge of HSK 2.0 and was not re-verified this session.
- The two-plays-per-item finding comes from measured segment lengths, not an official statement. It is very consistent, though: identical durations to 0.01 s across dozens of items.

### Gaps
- None of the official documents states in words how many times the audio is played; that comes from my audio analysis. HSK 5–6 audio was not analyzed (HSK 4 was played once).
- I could not confirm whether the answer-sheet transfer time also exists in the computer-based (iBT) mode.
- I could not determine whether the 22 Nov 2026 HSK 7–9 session uses the 98-item format or the 91-item format from the 2025 sample pack.
- The contents of the ≈27.5 s audio introductions and of the per-part spoken instructions were not transcribed (no speech-recognition tool was available).

## 2. Official syllabus components (任务 / 话题 / 词汇 / 汉字 / 语法): what each level contains

### Takeaway
The «HSK考试大纲» (CLEC, Nov 2025) has five components. For HSK 1 / 2 / 3 they are:
- tasks: 15 / 17 / 22 task categories;
- topics: 5-15-30 / 5-18-34 / 6-22-54;
- vocabulary: 300 / +200 / +500 words (cumulative 300 / 500 / 1,000);
- characters to recognize: 246 / +125 / +284 (cumulative 246 / 371 / 655);
- characters to write: 100 for HSK 1–2 combined, +150 at HSK 3 (cumulative 250);
- grammar: 70 / 78 / 96 items.

Syllabus-wide totals are 11,000 words, 3,088 characters to recognize, 1,200 characters to write, 593 grammar items and 427 third-level topics. The last two match figures cited by a test centre, which confirms the counting method.

### Inferences
- An app can build its HSK 1–3 content (vocabulary, characters to recognize and write, grammar tags, topic and task tags) entirely from DAGANG. The counts above work as completeness checks: 300 / 500 / 1,000 words, 246 / 371 / 655 characters to recognize, 100 / 250 to write, 70 / 78 / 96 grammar rows.
- The two independent matches (593 grammar items, 427 topics) suggest the official way of counting is one grammar item per table row and one topic per third-level topic.
- Characters to write are first examined at HSK 2 (HSK 1 has no writing section), even though the writing list is shared by HSK 1–2.

### Gaps
- The syllabus prints no per-level totals for tasks or grammar; those are my counts. My ≈678 can-do statements versus the 672 cited by HSK GTA is unresolved, possibly because of how statements split across pages are counted.
- Not re-verified this session: the word lists of the earlier 2021 «国际中文教育中文水平等级标准» (widely circulated as "HSK 3.0") have different per-level sizes from the 2025 syllabus. Apps built on 2021 lists should be re-checked against DAGANG.

## 3. Scoring, pass thresholds, score reports, validity

### Takeaway
As of 19 Oct 2026 I found **no official score scale or pass mark for HSK 3.0 levels 1–6**. The official notices give only result release dates and the two-year validity. chinesetest.cn's level pages still show HSK 2.0 scoring (HSK 1: 200 points, pass 120; HSK 3: 300 points, pass 180). The written HSK and the paired speaking test have separate result dates, which suggests separate results.

### Cited Findings
- HSK 2.0 scoring is still what chinesetest.cn's level pages show. HSK 1 reports "three scores: listening, reading, and total", full score 200, pass "120 or above", valid two years — [CTI HSK/1](https://www.chinesetest.cn/HSK/1). HSK 3 reports listening, reading, writing and total, full score 300, pass 180 — [CTI HSK/3](https://www.chinesetest.cn/HSK/3)
- First HSK 3.0 pilot (31 Jan 2026): scores released 14 March 2026, "valid for two years" — [CCTV, 19 Dec 2025](https://edu.cctv.com/2025/12/19/ARTIV4R5P66203aDjA16v9rn251219.shtml). No diagnostic analysis report was provided for this pilot — [hskmock mirror of the pilot announcement](https://www.hskmock.com/en/blog/303.html)
- Second pilot (20 Sep 2026): "机网考结束后15天、纸笔考结束后30天" (results 15 days after the computer-based test, 30 days after the paper test, one week later if public holidays intervene) — [CTI notice page](https://www.chinesetest.cn/notice)
- 13 Dec 2026 session, official calendar: paper results 15 January 2027 (marked as delayed by holidays); computer-based HSK results 28 December 2026; computer-based HSK Speaking Test results 4 January 2027 — [CTI 2026 test calendar](https://www.chinesetest.cn/news/50278989)
- The official launch notice (9 Sep 2026) does not mention score scales, pass marks or certificate changes — [CTI launch notice](https://www.chinesetest.cn/news/51336883)
- Secondary sources agree that nothing has been published:
  - studycli (14 Jul 2026): "No specific pass marks provided", scores "valid for two years" — [studycli](https://studycli.org/hsk/the-new-hsk/)
  - recabulary (3 Aug 2026): scoring scales, pass marks and score-report format not addressed — [recabulary](https://www.recabulary.com/blog/hsk-3-0-vs-hsk-2-0/)
  - CI Barcelona: scoring not specified; HSK 2.0 certificates remain valid two years — [CI Barcelona](https://www.confuciobarcelona.cat/zh/exams/cambios-hsk)
  - mandarinzone's scoring guide (modified 16 Jun 2026) covers only HSK 2.0 (60% rule) — [mandarinzone scoring](https://www.mandarinzone.com/official-hsk-scoring-system-and-passing-scores-explained-2024/)

### Inferences
- The app should not present a pass mark for HSK 3.0 as official. If it shows one, it should label HSK 2.0's 60% rule (120/200, 180/300) as a provisional reference only.
- The 200-point, listening-plus-reading scheme of old HSK 2 cannot carry over unchanged, because new HSK 2 has a writing section. Some search-engine summaries apply HSK 2.0's "HSK 1–2 = listening + reading = 200 points" to HSK 3.0, which mixes the two versions.
- The separate result dates for "HSK" and "HSK Speaking Test" suggest separate score reports for the written and speaking exams.

### Gaps
- Unknown: the official HSK 3.0 score scale per level, scores per section, pass marks, whether certificates show pass/fail, and how speaking is scored.
- Two claims seen only in search-result summaries could not be traced to a specific page: "CTI and CLEC hadn't published pass marks as of 6 October 2026", and "speaking and written scores are listed separately; failing speaking does not cancel a written pass".
- New HSK 7–9 scoring was not researched.

## 5. Test modes (paper vs internet-based vs home) and the computer-based interface

### Takeaway
HSK 3.0 is offered on paper and by computer at a test centre. No home version of HSK 3.0 has been announced, although CTI lists an "at home" internet test among its general HSK formats. The official demo client ("网考客户端V3.0", Chrome-only) loads its own pinyin input method, a drag-and-drop module and a voice recorder. Login uses the admission number and an exam password, preceded by camera, microphone, headphone and network checks.

### Cited Findings
- General HSK formats on chinesetest.cn: "Paper-based Test; Internet-based Test (at center); Internet-based Test (at home)" — [CTI About HSK](https://www.chinesetest.cn/hsk)
- HSK 3.0 pilots and the launch offer only paper and computer-based tests — [CCTV](https://edu.cctv.com/2025/12/19/ARTIV4R5P66203aDjA16v9rn251219.shtml); [CTI notice page](https://www.chinesetest.cn/notice); [CTI launch notice](https://www.chinesetest.cn/news/51336883). Every HSK 1–6 date in 2026 is "PBT+iBT at-center", and HSK 7–9 is "iBT at-center" — [CTI 2026 test calendar](https://www.chinesetest.cn/news/50278989)
- The chinesetest.cn homepage has a "Try HSK 3.0" demo with two entry points — [CTI homepage](https://www.chinesetest.cn/); [demo (international)](https://hsk3demo-intl.chinesetest.cn); [demo (China)](https://hsk3demo.chinesetest.cn)
- Demo client: "网页版考试仅支持Chrome浏览器"; "网考客户端V3.0 版权所有 © 教育部中外语言交流合作中心 / 汉考国际" — [demo welcome page](https://hsk3demo-intl.chinesetest.cn/Html/welcome.html)
- Pre-exam checks: environment check, "摄像头和麦克风检测" (camera and microphone), "耳机检测" (headphones), "网络检测" (network speed); there is also a variant without a camera — [demo env_hsk](https://hsk3demo-intl.chinesetest.cn/Html/env_hsk.html); [demo env_hsk_no_camera](https://hsk3demo-intl.chinesetest.cn/Html/env_hsk_no_camera.html). Microphone setup: "开始录音 / 播放录音 / 请调节音量，至适合大小" — [demo envOralVoice](https://hsk3demo-intl.chinesetest.cn/Html/envOralVoice.html)
- Login: 所属考点 (test centre), 准考证号 (admission number), 考试密码 (exam password). The candidate must tick "我已阅读考场须知" (has read the rules) and accept a "考试保密承诺书" (confidentiality pledge: no photos, no recording of item content) — [demo login](https://hsk3demo-intl.chinesetest.cn/Html/login.html). Interface languages: English, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Thai, Vietnamese, Mongolian, Spanish — [demo mlogin](https://hsk3demo-intl.chinesetest.cn/Html/mlogin.html)
- The login pages load `simple-input-method/simple-input-method.js`, `pinyinUtil.js` and `dict/pinyin_dict_notone.js` (a built-in toneless pinyin input method), plus `dragdrop.js`, `mp3recorder/recorder.js` and `voice.js`. A source comment mentions "右下角的输入法、音量和关闭按钮" (input method, volume and close buttons at bottom right) — [demo login source](https://hsk3demo-intl.chinesetest.cn/Html/login.html); [demo mlogin source](https://hsk3demo-intl.chinesetest.cn/Html/mlogin.html)
- The pre-login logic distinguishes "模拟考试" (mock exam) from "正式考试" (real exam) — [demo prelogin](https://hsk3demo-intl.chinesetest.cn/Html/prelogin.html)
- Press report (secondary): AI is being used for essay marking, proctoring and scoring, alongside adaptive learning products — [163.com / 上游新闻](https://www.163.com/dy/article/KH818PEN053469M5.html)

### Inferences
- On the computer-based test, written answers are probably typed with the client's own toneless pinyin input method (type pinyin, pick the character). The HSK 2–3 "write the character" items then become "recognize and select the correct character". The HSK 2 component-completion and matching items are probably answered by clicking or drag-and-drop. This is inferred from the client's code, not documented.
- A realistic app simulator would need:
  - an exam-client look (Chrome-style full screen, timer, part navigation);
  - an on-screen toneless pinyin input method;
  - audio that plays automatically;
  - drag-and-drop matching;
  - microphone recording with prep and answer countdowns for speaking;
  - pre-exam device checks.
- No home version of HSK 3.0 should be assumed.

### Gaps
- The item screens are behind a demo login (admission number and password), so I could not see:
  - whether audio can be paused or replayed;
  - whether candidates can move back and forth between items;
  - how HSK 2 component items and written answers are actually entered (typing vs handwriting pad);
  - whether answer-sheet transfer time exists on the computer test.
- No official statement on a home version of HSK 3.0 was found, either way.

## 6. Speaking: HSK 口语 for levels 3–6, HSKK, and HSK 7–9

### Takeaway
For HSK 1–6, speaking is not part of the written exam. Instead, HSK 3.0 brings new **level-specific speaking tests** (HSK 三级/四级/五级/六级 口语), and candidates for HSK 3–6 must register for the written and speaking tests together. Level 3 is 15 items in about 15 minutes. Levels 4–6 are 5 items each, in about 20, 23 and 23 minutes. At HSK 7–9, speaking is a section inside the exam itself.

### Cited Findings
- **HSK（三级）口语:** "分三部分，共15题：1．听后重复（8题） 2．看图说话（5题） 3．回答问题（2题）"; "全部考试约15分钟（含准备时间6分钟）".
  - Timings: repeat after hearing, 10 s per item; describe a picture, 15 s per item; answer questions, 3 min preparation and 1.5 min answer each.
  - Sample questions: "请介绍一下你的一位家人。" "你喜欢做什么样的工作？为什么？"
  - Source — [CTI sample pack: HSK口语/HSK（三级）口语样卷.pdf pp.1–2](https://hsk-sample-1409982902.cos.ap-beijing.myqcloud.com/HSK3.0.zip)
- **HSK（四级）口语:** 5 items: 听后复述 (retell what you heard) 2 items, 40 s each; 看图说话 1 item, 3 min preparation + 2 min answer; 回答问题 2 items, 3.5 min preparation + 2 min answer; "全部考试约20分钟（含准备时间10分钟）". Sample: "如果给你七天假，你打算怎么安排？" — [CTI sample pack: HSK（四级）口语样卷.pdf](https://hsk-sample-1409982902.cos.ap-beijing.myqcloud.com/HSK3.0.zip)
- **HSK（五级）口语:** 5 items: 听后复述 2 items, 1.5 min each; 看图说话 1 item, 3 + 2 min; 回答问题 2 items, 3.5 min preparation + 2.5 min answer; about 23 min including 10 min preparation. Sample: "人们常说：'活到老，学到老。'你是怎么理解这句话的？" — [CTI sample pack: HSK（五级）口语样卷.pdf](https://hsk-sample-1409982902.cos.ap-beijing.myqcloud.com/HSK3.0.zip)
- **HSK（六级）口语:** same layout and timings as level 5, about 23 min. Sample: "有人说'最好的投资就是投资自己'，你赞成这个观点吗？" — [CTI sample pack: HSK（六级）口语样卷.pdf](https://hsk-sample-1409982902.cos.ap-beijing.myqcloud.com/HSK3.0.zip)
- The official HSK 3 speaking sample audio lasts 980 s (≈16.3 min) — [CTI sample pack: HSK口语/HSK（三级）口语样卷.mp3](https://hsk-sample-1409982902.cos.ap-beijing.myqcloud.com/HSK3.0.zip)
- Registration rule in both pilots: HSK 3–6 must be registered together with the speaking test of the same level — [CCTV](https://edu.cctv.com/2025/12/19/ARTIV4R5P66203aDjA16v9rn251219.shtml); [CTI notice page](https://www.chinesetest.cn/notice). The official calendar is headed "Chinese Proficiency Test/HSK (Levels 1-6)+HSK Speaking Test" and gives separate computer-based result dates for "HSK" and "HSK Speaking Test" — [CTI 2026 test calendar](https://www.chinesetest.cn/news/50278989)
- At HSK 7–9, speaking is integrated: 5 items, about 20 min, inside the roughly 200-minute exam — [CTI sample pack: HSK（七—九级）样卷.pdf p.1](https://hsk-sample-1409982902.cos.ap-beijing.myqcloud.com/HSK3.0.zip). CI Barcelona: "口语部分已纳入考试的一部分" for 7–9, while lower levels keep separate speaking tests aligned with written levels (HSK 3, 4, 5, 6) — [CI Barcelona](https://www.confuciobarcelona.cat/zh/exams/cambios-hsk)
- For comparison, the old HSKK (Advanced) format: "听后复述（3题，7分钟） 2．朗读（1题，2分钟）…" — [old HSKK advanced sample, chinesetest.cn](https://www.chinesetest.cn/userfiles/file/HSK-gaoji.pdf) (seen as a search-result excerpt)
- Secondary: the old three-band HSKK (Elementary / Intermediate / Advanced) "still matters", with the new rules applied "not for every test date or test center at once" — [mandarinzone HSKK guide, updated 17 Jun 2026](https://www.mandarinzone.com/hskk-all-you-need-to-know/)
- Italy, under HSK 2.0 in 2026: HSKK is mandatory with HSK 3–6 (Elementary for 3, Intermediate for 4, Advanced for 5–6) — [CI Bologna](https://site.unibo.it/istitutoconfucio/it/certificazioni/l-hsk-chinese-proficiency-test); [CI Roma](https://www.istitutoconfucio.it/certificazioni-internazionali/hsk-2/)

### Inferences
- Under HSK 3.0, the one-test-per-level speaking exams appear to replace the three-band HSKK for levels 3–6, with Elementary/Intermediate/Advanced HSKK continuing only alongside HSK 2.0. CI Barcelona's wording suggests this, but CTI has not stated it explicitly.
- An app needs four speaking simulators (levels 3–6) with exact preparation and answer countdowns and recording. Level 3 also needs fast "repeat after hearing" drills (10 s) and quick picture descriptions (15 s). Levels 4–6 also need retelling after listening (40 s at level 4, 1.5 min at levels 5–6).
- The speaking test is not printed for HSK 1–2, and there is no sign of a speaking test at those levels.

### Gaps
- Unknown: speaking scoring scale and pass mark; whether a speaking result is required for an HSK 3–6 certificate; whether stand-alone HSKK (without the written HSK) remains available after Dec 2026.
- The audio of the speaking samples was not analyzed beyond its total length.
